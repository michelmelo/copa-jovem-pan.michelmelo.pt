# Taste
- In commercial proposals, keep the investment/price out of the opening sections — present it only at the end (penultimate/final section), never on the cover. Confidence: 0.85
- Wants deliverables to look polished and professional, explicitly invoking UX/UI design principles (strong visual hierarchy, refined cover, summary/TOC, numbered section headers, consistent spacing and rhythm). Confidence: 0.7
- Flags gradient effects that render poorly (banding, blotchy/uneven color) and dislikes them; prefers clean flat solid colors with crisp-edged geometric accents over soft gradients in print/PDF designs. Confidence: 0.6
- Wants proposals/documents to include concrete visual examples of the actual product — real screenshots/mockups of the working site rather than abstract illustrations or text-only descriptions. Confidence: 0.55
- Actively adds external tooling (e.g. MCP servers installed via npx/npm) to extend the agent's capabilities and raise the quality of design/UX work. Confidence: 0.55
- Communicates in Portuguese (pt-BR) and expects work products (docs, UI copy) in that language. Confidence: 0.6
